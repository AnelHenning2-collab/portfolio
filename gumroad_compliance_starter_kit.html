Document 01

HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices — Small Dental/Medical Practice

What it does: A plain-language notice explaining how your practice may use and disclose protected health information (PHI), accurate to 45 CFR §164.520, written so patients actually understand it.

Excerpt: "This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed, and how you can get access to this information. We are required by law to maintain the privacy of your protected health information, provide you with this notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and notify affected individuals following a breach of unsecured PHI."
