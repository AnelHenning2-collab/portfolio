01
Research & Wireframes
Starting From the Real Problem

The Concept

TransitionTrack started from a personal need: managing a career pivot generates dozens of scattered tasks — applications, certifications, networking follow-ups — with no single place to see progress. Early wireframes focused on one job: turn a messy transition into a visible, trackable list.

In My Own Words

"I designed this because I needed it. Every screen started as a sketch of my own actual to-do list, then got generalized once the pattern held up for someone else's transition too."

02
UI Design System
Designing the Task Dashboard

The Concept

The dashboard needed to make progress visible at a glance — completed tasks, open tasks, and upcoming deadlines, without feeling like a corporate project-management tool. The visual system uses a warm indigo-and-gold palette to feel personal rather than enterprise.

In My Own Words

"Most project-management tools feel built for a team, not a person going through something hard. I wanted this one to feel calm and encouraging, not like another spreadsheet."

From Prototype to Working Scaffold

The Concept

Beyond the interactive Figma prototype, TransitionTrack has a working desktop-app scaffold in progress, built to eventually package as a real installable application rather than stay a design exercise.

In My Own Words

"Designing an app and actually building one are different disciplines. I wanted to push this past the prototype stage and see it become something real."
