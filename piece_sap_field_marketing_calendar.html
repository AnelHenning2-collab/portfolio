Regional Enterprise Event Campaign Brief & Content Calendar

A spec campaign brief for a traveling enterprise field-marketing event — the kind of pre/during/post structure that turns a single-day event into a multi-week content campaign.

What this is: A speculative planning-template sample, not client work for or commissioned by SAP. I built it using a generic enterprise field-marketing scenario to demonstrate campaign planning and content-calendar structure, not to represent an actual SAP program.

Why I built this for SAP's field marketing team

Chris Durkin has worked as Marketing Director on SAP's regional field tour programs, and Keith Schmehl runs North America Field Marketing strategy and execution out of the Office of the CMO. Both roles live at the intersection of event logistics and sustained content output — a single regional event only pays off if it's wrapped in weeks of promotion and follow-up content, not treated as a one-day expense. This brief shows I can build that structure end to end.

Campaign Brief
Campaign
Regional "Business Reimagined"-style enterprise roadshow, single city stop
Audience
Mid-market and enterprise IT/operations decision-makers within driving distance of the venue
Objective
Fill the room, then convert attendance into a sales-qualified pipeline within 30 days of the event
Primary Channels
Email nurture, LinkedIn organic + paid, landing page, sales outreach enablement
