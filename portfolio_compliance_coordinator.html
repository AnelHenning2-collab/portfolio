Sample 01 — Policy Excerpt

HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices — Small Dental Practice

Purpose: Plain-language notice explaining how a covered entity may use and disclose protected health information (PHI), written for a non-technical audience (patients) while remaining accurate to 45 CFR §164.520 requirements.

Excerpt: "This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed, and how you can get access to this information. We are required by law to maintain the privacy of your protected health information, provide you with this notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and notify affected individuals following a breach of unsecured PHI. We will not use or share your information other than as described here unless you tell us we can in writing."
