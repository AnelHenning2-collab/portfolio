Sample 03 — Control Narrative

Control Narrative Excerpt — User Access Provisioning

"New user accounts are requested by the hiring manager via the standard access request form and require written approval from the system owner before provisioning. Access is granted at the minimum level required for the employee's role (least privilege). IT confirms provisioning within one business day and logs the request, approval, and grant date in the access log. This control addresses the risk of unauthorized or excessive access being granted at onboarding."

Sample 08 — AI Agent Governance & Risk Assessment

Governance Framework for Agentic AI Tools (Claude Code) — Mapped to NIST AI RMF

Built from direct hands-on use of agentic AI (Claude/Claude Code, Perplexity) in compliance documentation work — applying the same GRC discipline to the AI tools performing the work as to the systems being audited.

NIST AI RMF FunctionControl AppliedStatus
GOVERNDocumented scope of what the agent may act on (file edits, research) vs. what requires human approval (sending, publishing, purchasing)In place
MAPRisk categorization of agent tasks — low-risk (drafting, research) vs. high-risk (external communication, financial, credential handling)In place
MEASUREReview of agent output for accuracy before use (e.g., verifying sourced claims, checking generated content against source documents)Ongoing
MANAGEHuman-in-the-loop checkpoint before any irreversible or externally-visible action; activity log retained for traceabilityIn place

Why this matters: As agentic AI tools become embedded in compliance and audit workflows, the ability to govern the tool — not just use it — is an emerging GRC skill. This artifact demonstrates that oversight applied in practice, not just studied.
