How it fits together

Cybersecurity Consulting & GRC Architecture

Cyber risk intelligence links governance decisions to the security controls that defend the business. Risk scores and threat context flow in one direction; what to harden and detection data flow back.

Governance, Risk & Compliance
NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO/IEC 27001 framework, a scored risk register with owners, yearly-reviewed policies, and HIPAA, SOC 2 and PCI DSS mapping.
Cyber Risk Intelligence
Collect from CISA advisories, the KEV catalog and Health-ISAC; check the threat against your assets; raise or confirm the risk score; set a dated fix and automate reporting.
Security Controls
Data protection, identity and access (MFA, least privilege, reviews), cloud configuration and vendor oversight, and network and endpoint patching and monitoring.

Design for cost

Print Redesign: Line Drawings Instead of Color Blocks

The screen versions use dark covers and filled panels. For printing, every large color block becomes an outline in the same color, and any text that sat on a dark block turns to dark ink. Layouts, charts and wording stay the same. The same idea runs through the book: spend a little thinking early to save cost later.

01
Blocks become outlines
Filled cards, bars and table headers keep their shape and color as a thin line drawing.
02
Full-bleed color goes
Dark covers and header bands become white paper, with a single rule where the band ended.
03
Text stays readable
White text that sat on a dark block turns to navy ink. Small accents such as bullets and thin rules stay solid.
Bayside Fresh Market · cover 96% less ink
The full-page navy cover becomes white, and the truck icon becomes a line drawing.
Executive summary 78% less ink
The navy header band and the "Why Now" panel become outlines; tinted side cards keep only a border.
CyberTech|X deck · 90-day goals slide 97% less ink
Dark slides print on white; the teal numbers carry the color.
Pitch deck · slide 7 76% less ink
The gray slide background goes, and the "Pre-revenue" panel becomes an outline.
Bayside Fresh Market · results and charts 38% less ink
Text-heavy pages save less, but the bar charts become clean line drawings.

Coverage is the average darkness of each rendered page, a rough stand-in for toner or ink use. Actual savings depend on the printer.
