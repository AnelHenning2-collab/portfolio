The Challenge
One Letter or Number a Day, 36 Days Straight

The Concept

36 Days of Type runs the alphabet first — A through Z, one letterform per day — then the numbers 0 through 9. Each piece in this set draws on a favorite artist's style, worked out live during Adobe Live streams with Photoshop and Dimension. Every letter carries its own internal pattern or texture rather than a flat fill, so the set doubles as a study in surface design as much as letterform design.

In My Own Words

"Doing this live on Adobe Live changed the discipline of it — there's no going back to fix a letter once the stream moves on. Thirty-six days of committing to a mark and living with it."

Why It Matters
Practice, Live and In Public

The Concept

Participating in 36 Days of Type through Adobe Live meant working in front of an audience in real time, with Photoshop and Dimension as the working tools — a different discipline than refining a piece in private. The finished set of 36 letters and numbers, plus the wallpaper backgrounds built alongside them, remains one of the clearest records of that period's illustration and pattern-design practice.
