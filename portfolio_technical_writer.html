Sample 01 — Standard Operating Procedure

SOP: Quarterly Access Review (Excerpt)

  1. Purpose. Confirm that user access to systems containing sensitive data matches each person's current role.
  2. Scope. Applies to all systems storing PHI, financial records, or customer data.
  3. Procedure. Export the current user access list from each in-scope system. Compare against the HR roster of active employees and current job roles. Flag any account belonging to a terminated employee, or any access level that exceeds what the role requires.
  4. Documentation. Record findings in the Access Review Report — one line per account, with reviewer name, date, and remediation status.
  5. Escalation. Flagged accounts are disabled within 24 hours and reported to the system owner.

Before / After — Turning a Dense Technical Paragraph into Plain Language

Before
