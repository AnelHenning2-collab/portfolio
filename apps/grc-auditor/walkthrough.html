How a medical office and Logical Coders work together in one app

The practice gathers what we ask for, submits it and tracks the review. Logical Coders checks every file, runs the 12 templates and automated tests, and delivers the finished report. Every screen below is the working app, using fictional Sunshine Dental Group data.

Part 1 · For medical and dental office staff

Gather the evidence, submit it, track it

1

Choose your portal and sign in

Office staff use the Practice portal. Logical Coders auditors use the staff portal. Each sees only their own screens.

  • Work email and password
  • No shared logins: every person has an account
2

Confirm it's you with a 6-digit code

Every sign-in asks for a code from an authenticator app. This second step is the same safeguard the audit checks for at the practice.

3

See your four steps

The home screen shows exactly what to do next: profile, evidence, submit, track. The current step is highlighted.

4

Tell us about the practice

Staff count, offices, who manages the computers, and which systems hold patient information. This decides which safeguards we test. No patient data is asked for.

5

Upload each item, or mark it not applicable

Each request says what is needed, when it's due, and which template and control it supports. A warning reminds staff never to upload patient records or passwords.

6

Check the package and submit

A summary shows every item and file. The office confirms the files are accurate and contain no patient records, then sends the package to Logical Coders.

7

Track the review

A timeline shows where the review stands, and an activity log records every step with a date. The office never has to call and ask "where are we?"

Part 2 · For Logical Coders

Review, test and deliver

8

See every client in one list

After signing in (with the same 6-digit check), the auditor sees all engagements. New submissions are flagged.

9

Check each file and accept or return it

The auditor confirms receipt, runs the seven acceptance checks on each file, and accepts it or returns it with a note. The office sees the note right away.

10

Run the automated account review

The staff list is compared with system accounts using fixed rules. It finds the same 7 exceptions every time, including a former employee who kept access for 19 days.

11

Score the risks

Each threat to patient data is scored by likelihood and impact, before and after the fix, on a 5×5 heat map.

12

Publish findings, then close the engagement

The governance report is built from the register and tracker. The auditor publishes findings to the practice, then delivers the final package and closes the engagement.

Part 3 · The completed product

What the practice has at the end

13

Status: Complete

The timeline is finished and the activity log tells the whole story, from submission to delivery.

14

A one-page report leaders can act on

Overall posture, top risks before and after treatment, open findings, and the decisions the owner needs to make. Ready to print or save as PDF.

15

A to-do list in plain language

The practice sees only the fixes it owns, in plain words, with due dates. Ticking one off updates the tracker Logical Coders sees.

Prototype. All data is fictional; nothing leaves the browser. In production, sign-in, file storage and messages would run on a secure, access-controlled service under a Business Associate Agreement.
