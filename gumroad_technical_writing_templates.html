Template 01 — Standard Operating Procedure

SOP Template: Quarterly Access Review (Excerpt)

  1. Purpose. Confirm that user access to systems containing sensitive data matches each person's current role.
  2. Scope. Applies to all systems storing PHI, financial records, or customer data.
  3. Procedure. Export the current user access list from each in-scope system. Compare against the HR roster of active employees and current job roles. Flag any account belonging to a terminated employee, or any access level that exceeds what the role requires.
  4. Documentation. Record findings in the Access Review Report — one line per account, with reviewer name, date, and remediation status.
  5. Escalation. Flagged accounts are disabled within 24 hours and reported to the system owner.

The template gives you this exact five-part structure (Purpose, Scope, Procedure, Documentation, Escalation) blank and ready to fill in for any process you need to document.

Template 02 — Plain-Language Editing Checklist

Turning a Dense Technical Paragraph into Plain Language

Before
"Entities subject to the Security Rule must implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards commensurate with the risk analysis findings in order to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronically protected health information consistent with 45 CFR §164.306."
After
"If you handle patient data electronically, you need to put safeguards in place — policies, physical security, and technical controls — sized to the actual risks you find in your risk assessment. The goal: keep patient data private, accurate, and accessible when it's needed."

The checklist walks you through the same before/after process step by step, so you can apply it to your own dense policy language.
