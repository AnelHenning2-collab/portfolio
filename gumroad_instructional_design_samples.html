What the toolkit gives you

The reusable structure behind both modules

  1. Needs Assessment template — business context, the specific knowledge gap, why it matters, and real constraints (time, audience, jargon tolerance)
  2. Storyboard template — screen-by-screen structure: scenario → plain-language concept → knowledge check → feedback
  3. Evaluation plan template — Kirkpatrick Level 1–4 tracking (reaction, learning, behavior, results) so you can prove the training actually worked
