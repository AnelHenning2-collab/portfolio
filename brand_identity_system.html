Two Marks, One Discipline

Logical Coders and Logika Studios share one design system — the same rounded-square icon architecture, the same navy foundation — split into two registers by color, typography, and tone. This page documents both: logo lockups, palettes, type, voice, and usage rules.

Brand identity, illustration, fine art, product concept design.
Voice
  • Warm, considered, a little more expressive than Logical Coders
  • Speaks in finished work, not process jargon
  • Confident about range — 24 years across mediums

Do

  • Use the light-background icon on dark surfaces, dark-background icon on light surfaces
  • Keep clear space around the icon equal to the height of the "L"
  • Pair the icon with the wordmark at first mention; icon alone is fine after that
  • Keep navy as the shared anchor color across both brands

Don't

  • Recolor the icon outside its two approved palettes
  • Stretch, skew, or add a drop shadow beyond the standard card lift
  • Mix the two wordmark typefaces within a single lockup
  • Use the teal accent on Logika Studios or the slate accent on Logical Coders — that's the other brand's signal
